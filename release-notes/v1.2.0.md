## New

- **Check channels** in settings finds channels whose streams no longer work. TV Thing tries each channel's stream from your PC, marks the dead ones in red with the reason (such as "HTTP 404"), and offers to delete them all at once. **Undo** brings them back. Handy after importing a big playlist from iptv-org, where many links have gone stale.

## Install

Download TVThing-Windows-1.2.0.zip below and install it in the Bridgething desktop app over the previous version. Your channels and settings are kept.
