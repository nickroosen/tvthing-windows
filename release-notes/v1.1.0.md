## New

- **Delete all channels** in settings, for clearing out a big imported playlist. Click **Delete all…**, then click again within a few seconds to confirm. **Undo** puts the channels and their button assignments back.

## Fixed

- **Streams behind a redirect** (such as `jmp2.uk` links to Pluto TV channels) failed with "Upstream returned HTTP 404". The relay now resolves a playlist's links from where the redirect led, as VLC does.

## Install

Download TVThing-Windows-1.1.0.zip below and install it in the Bridgething desktop app over the previous version. Your channels and settings are kept.
