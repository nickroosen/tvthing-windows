## Fixed

- **Sound that falls far behind no longer makes the picture jump over and over.** When the sound on your PC stalls to buffer, the picture used to try catching up by jumping back every couple of seconds. The jumps could only go as far as the few seconds of video the Car Thing had stored, and jumping that often could crash the Car Thing and disconnect it. Now:
  - If the picture is further ahead than it can jump, it pauses for the rest of the gap (up to 10 seconds) so the sound catches up.
  - After a move that can't close the gap, it waits 20 seconds before trying again. Corrections are at least 6 seconds apart and at most 4 a minute.

## Install

Download TVThing-Windows-1.3.0.zip below and install it in the Bridgething desktop app over the previous version. Your channels and settings are kept.
